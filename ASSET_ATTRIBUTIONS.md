# Asset attribution and fork policy

The repository includes application icons, launch images, and visual assets
under `assets/`, `android/`, `ios/`, `macos/`, `web/`, and `windows/`.

They are supplied only to identify and build this source snapshot. The
source-code licence does not grant a separate right to use those assets as the
identity of a fork or distributed product. Fork maintainers must replace them
with assets they are authorized to use before public distribution.

See [BRANDING.md](BRANDING.md) for the corresponding naming and visual-identity
policy.
