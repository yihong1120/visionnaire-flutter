# Branding and visual assets

The source-code licence does not grant permission to use the `Visionnaire`
name, logo, icons, screenshots, or product-specific visual design as the name
or identity of a fork, deployment, service, or product.

Forks must use their own name, package identifier, signing identity, domains,
and deployment assets. They must replace the supplied visual identity before
public distribution unless they have separate written permission from the
relevant rights holder.

This policy does not claim ownership of marks or assets on behalf of any
person; it simply reserves them from the source-code licence.
